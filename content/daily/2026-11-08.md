LangChain LangGraph.js 项目｜发布：**2026-11-07 08:04:40–08:04:43**（00:04:40–00:04:43 UTC）｜首次发现：2026-11-08｜[SDK 1.15.0](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraphjs/releases/tag/%40langchain/langgraph-sdk%401.15.0) · [LangGraph 1.7.2](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraphjs/releases/tag/%40langchain/langgraph%401.7.2)

### 5. OpenTelemetry Collector Contrib 0.165.0 更新遥测兼容性

OpenTelemetry 项目｜Contrib 发布：**2026-11-07 10:04:10**（02:04:10 UTC）；发行包发布：**13:02:43**（05:02:43 UTC）｜首次发现：2026-11-08｜[Contrib 变更记录](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector-contrib/releases/tag/v0.165.0) · [发行包](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-collector-releases/releases/tag/v0.165.0)
